# Stakeholder Engagement & Change Management

**Simulated change-management case study | 500-employee manufacturer | SAP S/4HANA implementation**

This repository presents a stakeholder engagement and organizational change-management simulation for a fictional 500-employee manufacturer implementing SAP S/4HANA.

## Business problem

The ERP implementation creates new ways of working, conflicting departmental priorities, adoption risks and resistance from teams accustomed to existing processes. Successful delivery depends not only on technical configuration but also on stakeholder alignment, communication and sustained adoption.

## Simulated role

Change and Stakeholder Engagement Lead responsible for stakeholder analysis, communication planning, RACI definition, training coordination, resistance management and adoption measurement.

## Methodology

Stakeholder mapping, structured communication, RACI governance, change-impact assessment, training enablement and adoption measurement.

## Planned artifacts

- Stakeholder register
- Power-interest matrix
- RACI matrix
- Communications calendar
- Change impact assessment
- Training plan
- Resistance-management plan
- Adoption KPIs
- Decision log

## Decision-making simulation

Two senior stakeholders disagree on the rollout strategy. The case study will define evaluation criteria, assess the operational and adoption impact of each option, document the governance decision and explain how the chosen approach will be communicated.
