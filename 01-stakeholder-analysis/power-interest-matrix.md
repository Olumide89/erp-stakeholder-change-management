# Power-Interest Matrix

**Simulation scenario** | Stakeholder engagement strategy

## Matrix

|  | Low interest | High interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High influence** | Keep satisfied: CEO, External Auditor | Manage closely: COO, Finance Director, Operations Director, IT Director, HR Director, Implementation Partner |
| **Low influence** | Monitor: External Auditor | Keep informed: Procurement Manager, Production Managers, Finance Team, IT Support Team |

## Engagement actions

| Stakeholder group | Engagement approach |
|---|---|
| Manage closely | Involve in decisions, provide detailed updates, address concerns early, secure active sponsorship |
| Keep satisfied | Provide concise executive updates and escalate only material decisions or risks |
| Keep informed | Provide practical updates, training and opportunities to give feedback |
| Monitor | Provide compliance documentation and respond to assurance requests |

## Key engagement risk

Production Managers and parts of the Finance Team show resistance. They need early, practical involvement rather than top-down communication alone.
