# Decision Record — Rollout Strategy Disagreement

**Decision ID:** DEC-001  
**Decision owner:** Executive Steering Committee  
**Simulation scenario**

## 1. Situation

The Finance Director proposes a phased rollout starting with finance and procurement. The Operations Director prefers a big-bang rollout across all sites to avoid prolonged dual running and inconsistent data.

## 2. Options considered

| Option | Description | Advantages | Disadvantages | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Phased rollout | Implement finance and procurement first, then operations | Lower initial risk; time to learn and stabilize | Extended dual running; integration and data-consistency complexity | Viable but slower |
| B. Big-bang rollout | Implement all core functions and sites simultaneously | Avoids prolonged parallel running; consistent data model | Higher cutover and operational risk | Viable but higher risk |
| C. Hybrid rollout | Pilot one production site with finance and procurement, then scale in two waves | Tests critical processes; limits disruption; maintains momentum | Requires strong readiness criteria and coordination | **Recommended** |

## 3. Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Phased | Big-bang | Hybrid |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Operational disruption | 30% | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Data consistency | 25% | 3 | 5 | 4 |
| Adoption risk | 20% | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Delivery speed | 15% | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| Support readiness | 10% | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| **Weighted score** |  | **3.45** | **3.15** | **4.00** |

## 4. Decision

Adopt **Option C: a hybrid rollout**. Pilot finance, procurement and one production site first. Use the pilot to validate data quality, process design, training and support readiness before scaling to the remaining sites in two controlled waves.

## 5. Follow-up actions

- Define measurable pilot success criteria.
- Confirm wave sequencing and cutover dates.
- Assign adoption owners for each site and function.
- Review pilot results before approving Wave 2.
