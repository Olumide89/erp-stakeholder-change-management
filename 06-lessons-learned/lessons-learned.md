## Change-management checklist

- [ ] Stakeholders identified and segmented
- [ ] Engagement approach tailored to influence and interest
- [ ] RACI agreed
- [ ] Change impacts assessed by role
- [ ] Communications delivered and feedback collected
- [ ] Training completed and confidence measured
- [ ] Resistance addressed with named owners
- [ ] Adoption KPIs tracked after go-live
