# Adoption KPIs

**Simulation scenario** | SAP S/4HANA adoption measurement

| KPI | Definition | Target | Measurement frequency |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Training completion | Users completing role-based training | 95% before go-live | Weekly during training |
| System access | Users able to access relevant SAP roles | 100% before go-live | Weekly |
| Transaction adoption | Priority transactions completed in SAP rather than legacy tools | 90% within one month after go-live | Weekly |
| Spreadsheet workaround rate | Critical processes still managed outside SAP | Below 10% within one month | Weekly |
| Data quality | Critical master-data records meeting quality rules | 98% | Weekly |
| User confidence | Users reporting confidence using SAP | At least 80% | Before go-live and after 30 days |
| Support ticket trend | Incidents per 100 users | Declining trend after go-live | Weekly |
| Month-end close adherence | Close milestones completed on schedule | 100% in first two closes | Monthly |
