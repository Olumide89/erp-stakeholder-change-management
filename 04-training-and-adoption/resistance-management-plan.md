# Resistance Management Plan

**Simulation scenario** | SAP S/4HANA adoption

| Resistance driver | Likely group | Potential impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loss of familiar spreadsheet-based control | Production and finance teams | Workarounds and poor data quality | Explain benefits, involve users in design and provide practical support |
| Concern about job changes | Operational staff | Anxiety and disengagement | Clear role-impact communication and training |
| Lack of confidence with SAP | Key users | Low adoption and error rates | Hands-on training, sandbox practice and hypercare support |
| Perceived loss of approval control | Managers | Delayed approvals or parallel processes | Explain governance benefits and involve managers in workflow design |
| Change fatigue | Multiple teams | Reduced engagement | Sequence communications and avoid unnecessary meetings |

## Escalation approach

If resistance remains after targeted support, the Change Lead will meet the affected manager and key users, identify specific barriers, agree corrective actions, and escalate unresolved adoption risks to the steering committee.
