# Training Plan

**Simulation scenario** | SAP S/4HANA adoption

| Audience | Training | Format | Timing | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Key users | End-to-end process training and system navigation | Instructor-led and hands-on | Weeks 8–10 | Pass process assessment; able to complete key transactions |
| Finance team | Finance processes, reporting and month-end close | Role-based workshop | Weeks 9–10 | Complete simulated close activities |
| Production teams | Inventory transactions and approvals | Site-based practical training | Weeks 9–11 | Complete core transactions without support |
| Procurement team | Requisition, approval and supplier processes | Role-based workshop | Week 10 | Complete requisition-to-approval scenario |
| IT support | Troubleshooting, access and support procedures | Technical enablement | Weeks 10–11 | Resolve standard incidents using runbook |
| Managers | Change leadership and adoption monitoring | Briefing and dashboard walkthrough | Week 11 | Can explain changes and support their teams |

## Training principles

- Train using realistic, role-specific scenarios.
- Use key users as local champions.
- Provide quick-reference guides at the point of work.
- Track attendance, assessment results and post-training confidence.
- Offer refresher sessions during hypercare.
