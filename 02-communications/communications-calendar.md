# Communications Calendar

**Simulation scenario** | SAP S/4HANA implementation

| Audience | Communication | Frequency | Channel | Owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive steering committee | Programme status, risks, decisions and benefits | Monthly | Steering meeting and dashboard | Programme Manager | Strategic decisions and escalation |
| Business owners | Delivery progress, design decisions and adoption readiness | Bi-weekly | Working group | Programme Manager | Maintain alignment and resolve blockers |
| Key users | Process changes, training and feedback | Weekly | Workshop and newsletter | Change Lead | Build understanding and readiness |
| All employees | Why the change is happening and what will change | Monthly | Intranet and town hall | HR Director | Create awareness and reduce uncertainty |
| Production teams | Site-specific impacts and training schedule | Fortnightly | Site briefing | Operations Director | Address operational concerns |
| IT and support teams | Technical readiness, support model and cutover plan | Weekly | Technical governance meeting | IT Director | Ensure operational readiness |
| Implementation partner | Delivery progress, dependencies and issues | Weekly | Delivery governance meeting | Programme Manager | Manage delivery and quality |
