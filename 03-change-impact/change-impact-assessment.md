# Change Impact Assessment

**Simulation scenario** | SAP S/4HANA implementation

| Process area | Current way of working | Future way of working | Impact | Affected roles | Change level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Purchase requisitions | Email and spreadsheet approvals | SAP workflow with digital approvals | New approval process and audit trail | Procurement, operations | High |
| Invoice processing | Manual matching and email routing | Automated matching and workflow | Changed daily tasks and exception handling | Finance | High |
| Inventory management | Decentralized spreadsheets | Real-time SAP inventory transactions | New discipline and data-entry requirements | Production, warehouse | High |
| Financial reporting | Manual Excel consolidation | Standard SAP reports and dashboards | New reporting skills and data validation | Finance, management | Medium |
| Master data | Locally maintained records | Central governance and approval workflow | New ownership and quality controls | Multiple functions | High |
| Month-end close | Manual reconciliation | Standardized SAP close process | Changed sequence and deadlines | Finance | High |

## Change risks

- Employees may continue using spreadsheets if the new process is perceived as slower.
- Managers may resist losing local control over approvals.
- Inconsistent master data could undermine trust in SAP reports.
- Insufficient training could create errors during the first close period.
